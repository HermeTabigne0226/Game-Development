How to Play

  • Move: Hold WASD or Arrow keys for smooth real-time movement. Walk into chests, wells, shrines, anvils, stairs, and NPCs to interact!
  • Attack: Press J or Left Click on the map to strike or fire toward your cursor and smash crates/barrels.
  • Sub-Weapon Dash: Press Space to Dash (10 MP): Shield = Shield Bash slam & stun · Arrows/Quiver = Fires 3 Arrows (3 wall/enemy bounces) · Grimoire/Book = Blink Teleport + Arcane Burst!
  • Spells: Press 1–4 or click a spell icon to cast immediately toward your cursor.
  • Quick Potions: Press Q for Health Potion, E for Mana Potion.
  • Victory & Endless: Defeat the 3 Act Bosses (Floors 4, 8, and 12) to reforge the Crown, then continue into the Endless Abyss (Floors 13+) if you dare!

Multiplayer Guide

How to Play 1v1 PvP (Online & LAN)

Option 1 — Online PvP (Fastest & Easiest):

  • Stay on Online (Auto Cloud) — it connects automatically with zero setup.
  • One player clicks Create PvP Lobby; the 2nd player clicks Refresh → Join, picks a Hero, and clicks Ready Up.

Option 2 — PC ↔ Phone LAN (Same Wi-Fi):

  • Run the local server on your PC (node server.js). Open PvP Arena → LAN / Offline on your PC to see your PC LAN IP (e.g. 192.168.3.23).
  • On your Phone APK, switch to LAN / Offline, tap Scan Wi-Fi or type just that IP (192.168.3.23) and tap Connect IP (allow port 8080 in Firewall if prompted).

Option 3 — Phone ↔ Phone LAN (Offline Hotspot / Wi-Fi):

  • Connect both phones to the same Wi-Fi or turn on Mobile Hotspot on Phone A.
  • Phone A: Switch to LAN / Offline → tap Host LAN on Phone → Create Lobby.
  • Phone B: Switch to LAN / Offline → tap Scan Wi-Fi (or type Phone A's shown IP) → Join Lobby!
